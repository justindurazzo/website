Defender, the immersive configurator

A photorealistic, full-scale digital twin of the Defender you can build and walk around in mixed reality, streamed to the newest spatial headsets.

Year2024 to 2025
StudioAccenture Song
PartnersNVIDIA, Unreal Engine, Shutterstock
RecognitionRed Dot Award, 2025

Modern luxury buyers expect to touch a product before they commit. For the Defender, we built a way to do that without the physical vehicle in the room.

Photorealistic and physically accurate digital twins, built in NVIDIA Omniverse and Unreal Engine, let customers interact with a full-scale Defender through Apple Vision Pro and Meta Quest, changing it in real time and seeing every choice at true fidelity.

The same twin doubles as a content-production engine: more flexible, faster, and far cheaper than a traditional shoot, with Shutterstock generative tools extending the imagery. One asset, configured once, feeds both the customer experience and the campaign.
