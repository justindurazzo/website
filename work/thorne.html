Thorne: The Frontier Within

To relaunch a health brand, we pointed people at the one frontier they had never really explored: their own body, read live and rendered as a living inner landscape.

RoleCo-Creator and Experience Designer
Year2019
StudioDroga5, New York
ClientThorne
BuildReal-time WebGL, biometric input

Thorne makes premium supplements and at-home diagnostic tests, and it wanted to move from a practitioner favorite to a household name in luxury health without losing its scientific edge.

So instead of talking about the body from the outside, we let people travel inside it. The Frontier Within reads biometric signals and translates them, in real time, into a WebGL world you move through, your own data becoming the terrain.

Thorne and its audience share one goal: to defy the expectations of good health. The experience made that idea felt, turning an invisible act of self-knowledge into something you could see, hear, and wander through.
