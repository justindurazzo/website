Atlassian, the Dreamkeeper

An AI-powered website that turns the dreams you can barely remember into shareable visualizations you get to keep forever.

Year2023
StudioDroga5, New York
CampaignMake the Impossible Possible
TechChatGPT, Stable Diffusion

The Dreamkeeper is one piece of Atlassian's Make the Impossible Possible, a campaign built around a set of tasks that seem impossible to do alone. This one took on a strange one: holding on to a dream after you wake up.

On the site, a ChatGPT-powered dream assistant walks you through describing a dream, nudging loose the details you would otherwise lose. Stable Diffusion then reads what you have written and generates a unique visualization of it, so a thing that was slipping away becomes something you can save and share.

It was built with an unusual team for an ad: dream scientists alongside AI engineers, so the experience felt as credible as it did magical. Impossible alone, possible together, which was exactly the point Atlassian wanted to make.
