Mailchimp: Presents

An original content platform of short-form series and films made for entrepreneurs, built to connect with small-business owners on a deeper, more emotional level.

Year2019
StudioDroga5, New York
ClientMailchimp
FormatOriginal series and films

Mailchimp helps small-business owners connect with their customers. We saw a chance for the brand to connect with those owners on a more human level.

So we built Mailchimp Presents, a platform of entirely original content: short films and series spanning a range of stories and subjects, all reflecting the real, emotional texture of entrepreneurial life. Not ads about the product, but stories for the people who use it.

The goal was empathy: a shared sense that the ups and downs of running your own thing are worth telling stories about, and that the brand telling them actually understands the life.
