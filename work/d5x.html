Droga5: D5X

A one-day festival of music, art, food and play. Governors Island became a playground for 600 people on a long summer day.

Year2016
ClientDroga5
FormatOne-day festival, Governors Island
Guests600

To celebrate Droga5's tenth anniversary, we were asked to create a one-day festival that would be unforgettable.

We transformed Governors Island into a playground of music, art, food and play, an all-day thank-you to the people who built the agency and the people who believed in it.

The lineup ran from Ghostface Killah to Yeasayer, Kindness, Holy Ghost! and Diane Coffee, wrapped in art installations, food, and play across the island.
