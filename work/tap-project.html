UNICEF: Tap Project

Put your phone down to help save lives: for every ten minutes you resisted touching your phone, a sponsor funded a day of clean water for a child who needed it.

Year2014
StudioDroga5, New York
ClientUNICEF
RecognitionWebby Award, 2015

We spend a lot of the day looking at our phones. The Tap Project asked people to look away instead.

Visit the mobile site, put your phone down, and don't touch it. For every ten minutes you managed, a sponsor funded one day of clean water for a child through UNICEF. Doing nothing, for once, did something.

It turned a small act of restraint into real impact, and turned the device competing for your attention into the thing that gave it away. Recognized with a Webby Award in 2015.
