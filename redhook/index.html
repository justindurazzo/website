Our Story

The way it was supposed to be

Before 1870 A return to the roots of real bread.

Bread was simple, honest, and alive. Made with only flour, water, salt, and time. Slow fermentation, natural ingredients, bread you could feel good about.

1870 Convenience grew, quality declined.

The industrial revolution changed everything. Milling became faster, bread became cheaper, and with that progress came the loss of nutrients, flavor, and integrity.

1940 We lost something essential.

Processed foods took over. Additives, chemicals, and shortcuts became the norm. Bread became soft, uniform, and forgettable.

Today The way bread was meant to be.

We are bringing bread back to the table, the way it used to be. Clean label. High hydration. Long fermentation. Few ingredients, maximum digestibility.

Our Way This is The Red Hook Way.

At The Red Hook Focacceria we use a custom blend of 100% Italian semolina flour. Slow-fermented, handcrafted, honest, built on time, tradition, and care.
