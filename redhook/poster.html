Bread was simple, honest, and alive.
Made with only flour, water, salt, and time.
Slow fermentation. Natural ingredients.
Bread you could feel good about.

The industrial revolution changed everything.
Milling became faster. Bread became cheaper.
With progress came the loss of nutrients,
flavor, and integrity.

Processed foods took over. Additives,
chemicals, and shortcuts became the norm.
Bread became soft, uniform,
and forgettable.

We are bringing bread back to the table,
the way it used to be. Clean label.
High hydration. Long fermentation.
Few ingredients. Maximum digestibility.

At The Red Hook Focacceria we use a custom
blend of 100% Italian semolina flour.
Slow-fermented. Handcrafted. Honest.
Built on time, tradition, and care.
